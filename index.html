LiveShorts

A personal, self-hosted tool for producing YouTube Shorts.

LiveShorts runs only on its owner's own computer. It turns live gaming broadcasts that the owner is authorised to use into short vertical videos with original commentary. It uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API.

What it does with your YouTube account

  • Uploads videos you approve to the channel you choose. Every upload starts as private.
  • Reads your channel's ID and name, so it uploads only to the channel you confirmed.
  • Changes the privacy or schedule of videos it uploaded, when you ask it to.

It is not a public service. It has no user accounts, and nobody other than the owner uses it.
