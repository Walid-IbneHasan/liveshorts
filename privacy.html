Privacy policy

LiveShorts · effective 18 November 2026

LiveShorts is a personal, self-hosted application. It runs only on its owner's own computer and is used only by that owner. It has no servers of its own: no data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Google user data LiveShorts accesses

When you connect your YouTube account through Google sign-in, LiveShorts requests these permissions and uses them only as described:

PermissionUsed for
youtube.uploadUploading videos you have approved to your chosen channel.
youtube.readonlyReading your channel's ID and name, so uploads go only to the channel you confirmed. Listing your recent uploads to check whether an interrupted upload already completed.
youtube.force-sslChanging the privacy status or scheduled publication time of videos LiveShorts uploaded, when you ask it to (for example to cancel a scheduled publication).

LiveShorts never asks for or stores your Google password.

What is stored, and where

  • The Google sign-in token, in the operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager) on your computer.
  • Your channel ID and name, and the IDs and upload status of videos LiveShorts uploaded, in a local database on your computer.

Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Sharing

LiveShorts does not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. It does not use the data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or training AI or machine-learning models. LiveShorts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your computer until you delete it. To disconnect and delete the token:

  • Use Log out and revoke access on the YouTube page of the LiveShorts dashboard. This revokes the token at Google and deletes it locally straight away.
  • Or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting the LiveShorts data folder removes all locally stored information.

YouTube API Services

LiveShorts uses YouTube API Services. Your use of YouTube through LiveShorts is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
